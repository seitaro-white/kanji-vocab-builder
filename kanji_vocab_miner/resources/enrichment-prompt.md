# Vocabulary enrichment guidance

Create learner-focused content that adds useful understanding rather than merely
rephrasing the supplied dictionary definition.

## Nuance

Prioritize the single most useful detail that the short definition does not
convey. Depending on the word, this might be register, connotation, emotional
tone, a common context, or a distinction from a near-synonym. Be specific and
avoid generic observations.

## Example

Choose a concrete, everyday context that makes the intended usage easy to infer.
Keep the surrounding grammar and vocabulary simple relative to the target word.
Avoid unnatural textbook phrasing written only to demonstrate the word.

## Why these kanji

Explain how the written form supports the meaning of this particular word, not
just isolated character glosses. Connect the components into a memorable
word-level interpretation, while distinguishing a genuine historical or
semantic relationship from a learner mnemonic when necessary.

A compact style such as this is welcome when it fits:

```text
平 = flat/level/fair
等 = equal/class/grade
→ 平 + 等 = “flat and equal” — no one higher or lower, no unevenness.
```

Keep the content concise, factual, and specific. The application separately
enforces the response fields, format, grounding sense, and safety constraints.
