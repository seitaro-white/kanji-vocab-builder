# Vocabulary enrichment guidance

Enrich the supplied Japanese vocabulary word using only its primary dictionary sense and primary part of speech as the grounding context.

Provide these three pieces of content:

1. **Nuance** — Write one concise English sentence explaining how the word is used beyond its short dictionary definition. Focus on register, connotation, or a useful usage distinction.
2. **Example** — Write one short, natural Japanese sentence that uses the word in the grounded sense. Adapt the surrounding language to be simple and useful for a learner. Do not add an English translation or a separate reading. Use an exact surface form of the target word in the sentence.
3. **Why these kanji** — Explain at the word level what each kanji contributes to the word's meaning. For kanji with okurigana, explain the kanji together with that ending. For a single-kanji word, explain that kanji's contribution. If the word contains no kanji, state that plainly instead of inventing an analysis.

For example
```
- 平 = flat/level/fair
- 等 = equal/class/grade
   → 平 + 等 = "flat and equal" — no one higher or lower, no unevenness.
```

Keep every response concise, factual, and specific to the supplied word. The application will provide separate fixed instructions describing the required response format.
